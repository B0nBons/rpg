# Tales of Littleheart: An RPG

Hundreds of years ago, there was a town on the cusp of a forest. The forest was almost like a barrier, separating the town from the rest of the world. Despite this, the residents of Littleheart learned the tricks of the forest, and soon conquered it with help from a witch... ~~But as soon as the expedition of 1459 went missing...~~

## Discover A Dynamic World!

With five different main characters (2 female, 2 male, 1 non-binary), you're sure to find one who clicks with you! Each one has a distinct personality, from an oddly existential blacksmith, to an oddly hyper fisherman! As you make choices, watch as the story -- and what you learn -- changes! 

## Battles?!

Yep! Battles are (of course) present! Experience a fun, (soon to be) animated, turn-based fight! Random button mashing won't do you any good- only strategy will help you progress!
